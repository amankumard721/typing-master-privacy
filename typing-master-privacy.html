What the app stores and why

  • Practice profile and settings. The app stores the candidate name you enter and preferences such as your language and typing layout. It uses these to personalise practice and label results.
  • Practice text and drafts. Custom passages and free-typing drafts are saved locally so you can return to them. Drafts can include your typed text, cursor position, selected layout and active practice time.
  • Results and progress. The app saves typing speed, accuracy, practice time, lesson progress and related results to show your history and progress.
  • Optional font imports. If you import a font, the app uses Android’s document picker and copies only the selected file into its private storage.

Offline operation and third-party services

The app works offline and does not send your typing text, draft, candidate name or practice history to the developer. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics or developer-operated cloud storage.

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. External links, such as this privacy policy, can open in your browser. The website you visit and your browser handle that visit under their own privacy practices.

Sharing a practice result

If you choose to share a result, the app creates an image in its private cache containing your candidate name and typing statistics. Android’s share sheet lets you choose where to send it. The receiving app handles its copy under its own privacy policy. Deleting data in Typing Master does not delete copies you already shared.

Storage and security

Android’s private app storage holds your settings, drafts, imported fonts and practice history. The app’s backup rules exclude this data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. The app uses Android’s document picker for font import and does not request broad access to your files. Optional typing feedback uses the device’s vibration permission.

Retention and deletion

Local practice data stays on your device until you remove it or clear the app’s storage.

  • Reset progress removes practice history, lesson progress and daily totals.
  • New page clears the free-typing draft for the selected layout.
  • Clear cache in Android Settings removes cached result images from the app.
  • Clear storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove all of its local data.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests about your data, use the developer contact in the App support section of the Google Play listing for Typing Master: Exam Speed Test. If the app’s data practices change, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
