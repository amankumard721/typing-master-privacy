Privacy Policy for Typing Master: Exam Speed Test

Effective 14 November 2026.

Typing Master (formerly KeyTrail) is an offline Android app for Hindi and English typing lessons and speed tests. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or developer-operated cloud storage. Typing Master does not send your typing data to the developer.

Data stored on your device

Typing Master keeps your candidate name, preferences, custom passages, free-typing drafts, practice results, lesson progress, and daily practice totals in private app storage. Drafts can include what you typed, your typing layout, cursor position, and practice time. If you choose to import a font, Typing Master copies the selected file into private app storage. The app uses Android's file picker and does not request broad access to your files.

Sharing results

When you choose to share a result, Typing Master creates an image containing your candidate name and typing statistics in its private cache. Android's share sheet lets you choose a recipient app. Its privacy policy applies to any copy it receives.

Data retention and deletion

Typing Master keeps local data so you can resume practice and see your progress. Reset progress deletes practice history, lesson progress, and daily totals. Starting a new page clears the current layout's free-typing draft. Clearing Typing Master's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling it removes its local data. The app's backup rules exclude local data from Android backup and device transfer.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support details on the Google Play listing.
